Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into pieces
  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • ½ cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2–3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)

Instructions

  1. Marinate chicken in soy sauce and half of the garlic for 15–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil and sauté the remaining garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken and brown each side.
  4. Pour in the marinade and water.
  5. Add bay leaves and peppercorns.
  6. Bring to a boil.
  7. Add the vinegar. Don't stir for 2–3 minutes.
  8. Lower the heat and simmer for 25–35 minutes, or until the chicken is tender.
  9. Add sugar if you want a slightly sweeter adobo.
  10. Continue simmering until the sauce reaches your preferred thickness.
  11. Taste and adjust soy sauce, vinegar, or water.
  12. Serve hot with rice.
